# Texture attribution

Planetary color maps, Earth night lights and clouds: Solar System Scope / INOVE, https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/ . Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Maps are based on NASA data with artist adjustments and fictional fill in unmapped regions. Files are the published 2K JPEG maps, redistributed unmodified; Orrery applies lighting, clouds, exposure, and color processing at render time. Not current satellite observations.
